A Little About FlakyBubbles

For the love of paws.And their people.

At Flaky Bubbles Organic Luxury Pet Spa, we believe pets are family. Our mission is to provide a safe, clean, and luxurious home away from home where every pet is loved, nurtured, and treated with exceptional care. …

More than a grooming salon and boarding facility, Flaky Bubbles is a community where pets build friendships, enjoy structured socialization, and thrive in a calm, enriching environment. Our membership program encourages consistency and familiarity, helping pets feel comfortable with our team and with one another.

We proudly use premium, gentle, and organic products whenever possible because we believe every pet deserves the healthiest care without unnecessary harsh chemicals.

To ensure a relaxed and enjoyable experience, we require a minimum three-night boarding stay, allowing pets time to settle in, socialize, and truly enjoy their stay rather than simply passing through.

From luxury accommodations to personalized care, every detail at Flaky Bubbles is designed to give you peace of mind and your pet a vacation they’ll love.
