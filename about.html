A Little About FlakyBubbles

For the love of paws.And their people.

At Flaky Bubbles Organic Luxury Pet Spa, we believe pets are family. Our mission is to provide a safe, clean, and luxurious home away from home where every pet is loved, nurtured, and treated with exceptional care. …

More than a grooming salon and boarding facility, Flaky Bubbles is a community where pets build friendships, enjoy structured socialization, and thrive in a calm, enriching environment. Our membership program encourages consistency and familiarity, helping pets feel comfortable with our team and with one another.

We proudly use premium, gentle, and organic products whenever possible because we believe every pet deserves the healthiest care without unnecessary harsh chemicals.

To ensure a relaxed and enjoyable experience, we require a minimum three-night boarding stay, allowing pets time to settle in, socialize, and truly enjoy their stay rather than simply passing through.

From luxury accommodations to personalized care, every detail at Flaky Bubbles is designed to give you peace of mind and your pet a vacation they’ll love.

Our Story

More than pet care.A feeling of belonging.

Flaky Bubbles is built around a simple idea: pets are family, and their care should reflect that.

From a first grooming visit to a longer stay, our vision is to make everyday pet care feel welcoming, thoughtful, and easy for the people who love them.

We bring grooming, daycare, boarding, and transport together under one name, so you can explore care that fits your companion and your routine. At the heart of it all is a focus on comfort, connection, and the little details that matter.

What Matters To Us

Small details.A caring difference.

Our philosophy starts with seeing each pet as an individual—with their own personality, preferences, and pace.

01 / Comfort

Care at their pace.

We value a patient approach that puts a pet's comfort at the centre of the experience, from hello to home time.

02 / Connection

Know the little things.

A favourite game. A familiar routine. A note from you. Understanding what makes your pet unique is where thoughtful care begins.

03 / Trust

Keep people close.

You know your companion best. We believe clear conversations and listening to your needs are essential parts of their care.

04 / Organic Care

Thoughtfully chosen. Lovingly used.

Our care extends to the products we use. We choose organic grooming products as part of a thoughtful spa experience for your pet.
