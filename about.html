Our Genesis & Devotion

Crafted with Devotion, Inspired by Nature

FlakyBubbles was born from an unyielding insistence to eliminate harsh synthetic detergents, industrial cage dryers, and stress-inducing routines from pet wellness. Founded collaboratively by holistic veterinary dermatologists and devoted companions, we recognized a silent void in modern companion care.

We envisioned a serene haven guided by the standards of a five-star botanical retreat in Provence—where water is filtered through ionic minerals, essential botanical unguents are whipped fresh by hand, and every guest is honored as a sentient family member.
