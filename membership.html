Good to Know

Membership FAQs

Can I switch tiers later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time from your account, and the new rate applies from your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract?

No. Every Bubble Club plan is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime, no cancellation fee.

How is the discount applied?

Your tier's discount is applied automatically to the next service you book while your membership is active.

Do unused perks roll over?

Free nights, day passes and washes reset each month and don't carry over, so use them while they're active.

Ready to join the Bubble Club?

Pick a tier above, or ask us in person at your next visit — we'll set it up in minutes.
