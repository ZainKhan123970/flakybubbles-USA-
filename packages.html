Why Choose a Package?

01

Consistency is Key

Dogs thrive on routine and familiar environments. Packages ensure they come regularly, which reduces stress and maximizes socialization benefits.

02

Safer Playgroups

With a recurring group of familiar dogs, our handlers can manage energy, behavior, and compatibility more effectively.

03

Better Value for Owners

Packages are priced to reward commitment with meaningful savings.

04

Community Spirit

Flaky Bubbles is about building a trusted, friendly pack — not random daily drop-ins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our daycare and boarding packages, validity and how to get started.

A 10-visit package is valid for 2 months, a 20-visit package for 3 months and a 30-visit package for 4 months from the date of purchase. Please use all your visits within that window.

The 20-visit package saves you 5% and the 30-visit package saves you 10%. Switch between the 10, 20 and 30 visit options at the top of this page to see the exact price and the cost per visit for each service.

Packages are non-refundable and non-transferable, so please choose the size that fits your pet's routine. If you are unsure, our team is happy to help you pick the right one before you buy.

Bubble Half Day covers up to 5 hours, either 7 AM to 12 PM or 12 PM to 5 PM. Bubble Full Day covers up to 12 hours, from 7 AM to 7 PM. We also offer Pawcation Overnight boarding (7 PM to 7 AM) and After Pawty Evening Play (7 PM to 11 PM).

Pick your package and tap “Choose this package”. You will be taken to our booking portal, where you can log in, purchase the package and schedule your visits. You can also contact us if you need any help.
