Why Packages, Not Daily Passes

01

Consistency is Key

Dogs thrive on routine and familiar environments. Packages ensure they come regularly, which reduces stress and maximizes socialization benefits.

02

Safer Playgroups

With a recurring group of familiar dogs, our handlers can manage energy, behavior, and compatibility more effectively.

03

Better Value for Owners

Packages are priced to reward commitment with meaningful savings.

04

Community Spirit

Flaky Bubbles is about building a trusted, friendly pack — not random daily drop-ins.

A happy routine. A happy pup. A happy you.
