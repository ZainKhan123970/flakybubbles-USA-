Flaky Bubbles LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Flaky Bubbles LLC ("Flaky Bubbles," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our customers and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you visit our website, use our services, make appointments or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact information, including email address and phone number.
  • Pet information, including your pet's name, breed, age, grooming preferences and care requirements.
  • Appointment, grooming, daycare and boarding information.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Communication preferences and customer service correspondence.
  • Information submitted through our website, booking systems or customer forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Schedule and manage grooming, spa, daycare and boarding appointments.
  • Provide pet care updates and service notifications.
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders and pickup notifications.
  • Communicate important information about your pet's stay or services.
  • Process payments and manage customer accounts.
  • Respond to questions and customer service requests.
  • Send promotional offers and business updates where you have provided appropriate consent.
  • Improve our services and customer experience.

3. Text Messaging and SMS Privacy

Flaky Bubbles may send SMS text messages to customers who have provided their phone numbers and consented to receive messages.

These messages may include:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • Grooming progress and completion notifications.
  • Pet pickup and drop-off notifications.
  • Daycare and boarding updates.
  • Customer service communications.
  • Promotional messages and special offers, where separately authorized.

Message frequency varies depending on your services, appointments and communication preferences. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us using the information provided below.

We do not sell or share SMS consent or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We may share necessary customer information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business, including appointment booking platforms, payment processors, website hosting providers and communication service providers.

These providers may access information only as reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf and are expected to protect that information.

We do not sell customers' personal information.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent are excluded from these sharing arrangements and are not shared with third parties or affiliates.

5. Data Storage and Protection

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

Information may be stored electronically through secure business systems and service providers.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Information

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor activity and enhance the customer experience.

You may adjust your browser settings to manage cookies.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permissible.
  • Update your communication preferences.
  • Withdraw consent to promotional text messages at any time.

To stop SMS messages, reply STOP.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
