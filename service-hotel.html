Our Offer

FlakyBubbles Offer

At FlakyBubbles Organic Pet Spa, boarding is more than just an overnight stay — it's a personalized wellness experience designed for comfort, safety, and peace of mind. We offer individualized boarding in a calm, luxury environment where every pet receives one-on-one attention. Our space is 100% organic and chemical-free, carefully maintained using natural, non-toxic cleaning products to support sensitive skin, allergies, and overall well-being.

Quiet individual suites keep your pet calm, relaxed and away from noisy crowds.

A spacious, cozy suite with premium comforts for the most relaxing stay.

Camera monitoring helps our team keep a watchful eye on every pet's safety.

Meals, rest and play follow your pet's own habits, needs and preferences.

Quality meals served on a steady schedule, matched to your pet's diet.

Suites are cleaned and refreshed every day for a spotless, healthy space.

A balanced day of gentle play, mental stimulation and peaceful downtime.
